ProxyM Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ProxyM ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data remains secure. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension (ProxyM) handles your information when you install and use it.

Core Principle: ProxyM operates locally on your device. We do not track you, we do not log your browsing history, and we do not sell your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

ProxyM is designed to function with minimal data collection:

  • Local Storage: Your proxy configurations, rules, and settings are saved strictly within your browser's local storage via the standard chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.
  • No Personal Data: We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal identifiers (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
  • No Browsing Data: The extension routes your traffic according to your proxy rules but does not log, inspect, or record the websites you visit.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide advanced functionality like latency testing and proxy geo-location detection, ProxyM may make network requests to public third-party APIs (e.g., ipwho.is, freeipapi.com, ip.guide). When running an auto-detect or latency test:

  • The respective proxy's IP address may be shared with these APIs solely to determine its status and location.
  • These requests are standard HTTP calls and are subject to the privacy policies of the respective API providers.

4. Permissions Justification

To function correctly, ProxyM requires the following Chrome permissions:

  • Proxy: Required to route your browser traffic through the HTTP, HTTPS, SOCKS4, or SOCKS5 proxies you define.
  • Storage: Required to save your proxy list and application settings locally on your machine.
  • Alarms: Required to schedule background tasks, such as smart proxy rotation intervals.
  • Host Permissions: Required to bypass proxy routing for specific URLs and to verify proxy connectivity against public IP APIs.

5. Data Security

Since your configuration data resides locally within your browser environment, it is protected by the built-in security mechanisms of Google Chrome. We recommend keeping your browser updated and securing your device to prevent unauthorized access to your local data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
